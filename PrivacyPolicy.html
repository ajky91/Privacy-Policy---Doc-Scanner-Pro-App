Privacy Policy — Smart DocScanner Pro

Package: com.kums.smartdocscanner • Owner & Publisher: JamSoftwares • Updated: November 16, 2026

1. Ownership & Zero Data Collection

Smart DocScanner Pro belongs to JamSoftwares and is developed as a strictly offline-first document scanner and PDF studio. JamSoftwares does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information, scanned documents, photos, e-signatures, OCR text, or telemetry.

2. On-Device AI Vision & OCR

Multi-hypothesis document boundary detection, shadow-removal readability enhancement, Optical Character Recognition (OCR), and QR/barcode scanning run 100% locally on your Android processor with zero cloud uploads.

3. Scoped Access & Minimal Permissions

  • Camera: Used solely while actively scanning pages, ID cards, books, or QR codes.
  • Biometrics: Delegated to Android OS BiometricPrompt hardware keystore.
  • Files & Photos: Uses Android Photo Picker & SAF without broad storage permissions.

4. User-Controlled Exports & Encryption

Documents remain inside private app storage until you explicitly tap Share, Save to Downloads/Gallery, Print, or Export Portable ZIP Backup. Standard 128-bit PDF encryption runs locally on device.

Does DocScanner Pro send analytics or crash telemetry?

No. Telemetry is completely disabled by design (disable_telemetry=1). No third-party ad SDKs or analytics trackers are bundled in the APK/AAB.

Children's Privacy Compliance

Because the application collects zero personal data from any user, JamSoftwares does not knowingly or unknowingly collect data from children under 13.

Contact JamSoftwares

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Smart DocScanner Pro, contact JamSoftwares via the developer support email listed on Google Play.

Android Manifest Permission Audit

Smart DocScanner Pro requests only two hardware capabilities and zero background or storage permissions:

  • android.permission.CAMERA — Live document & QR capture
  • android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC — Optional fingerprint/face lock

Zero-Telemetry Guarantee

Built with disable_telemetry=1. Smart DocScanner Pro makes zero background network requests for analytics, profiling, or usage tracking.
